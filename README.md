# 🧠 PersonaSkill AI

A psychometric career intelligence tool that discovers your hidden natural talents through behavioral pattern analysis.

🔗 **Live App:** [personaskill-ai.streamlit.app](https://personaskill-ai.streamlit.app)

---

## 🎯 What It Does

- Asks 7 simple lifestyle-based questions
- Calculates your scores across 3 career intelligence categories
- Assigns a unique Persona Title based on your behavioral pattern
- Shows a personalized Career Roadmap with job roles and skills
- Generates a downloadable 3-page PDF report

---

## 🧠 Career Intelligence Categories

| Category | What It Measures |
|---|---|
| 📊 Analytical & Data | Detail spotting, pattern recognition, data thinking |
| ⚙️ Operations & Logic | Process optimization, system building, efficiency |
| 🚀 Strategy & AI-Thinking | Big-picture planning, foresight, AI-era decision making |

---

| Persona | Category | Score Range |

| 🚀 The AI Strategist | Strategy & AI-Thinking | 75–100 |
